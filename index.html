D-Juice Calculator   v1.12
× ◈ How to Use Juice Buddy ◈

JuiceBuddy tells you how to make your desired d-juice.

Say you need a total of 3.0ml of 1:4 d-juice using 70PG/30VG — adjust the top three sliders (deems ratio can be entered manually) to match these amounts, then read off the PG, VG and deems from the lower text fields.

To mix by a certain deems weight and don't know the final volume, adjust the bottom slider or edit the "Deems (mg)" field to match the weight you want to use.

A rough solubility guide (deems is insoluble in VG) is included. Note that the volume of PG increases significantly when deems is dissolved in it.

When mixing, suggest dissolving the deems in PG first (clean deems dissolves easily at room temp) and then adding the VG.

I find it much easier to measure PG and VG by weight, so these are included in the text fields.

Please report any bugs or suggestions to Minty via the Discord.
